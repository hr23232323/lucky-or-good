# 014 Real data: cleaning and scale
Status: done
Problem: the full MarketParquet archive (38M rows, 16,410 symbols) breaks two assumptions. (1) It has bad prints: 824 symbols jump more than 3x and snap back the next day, e.g. a $67,000 print in a $20 stock. A strategy holding through one earns thousands of percent, and the fake price inflates dollar volume, pulling broken names into a liquidity universe. (2) Pivoting every symbol for 26 years doesn't fit in memory, and every CLI call reloaded everything.
Outcome: the loader drops spike-and-revert bars (close or open more than 3x away from the previous close and reversing by more than 2x) and non-positive prices, in DuckDB. With a universe size set, DuckDB pre-selects candidates (ever in the top 2x by trailing median dollar volume) and only those are pivoted; the exact universe is then computed in pandas as before. The judge caches each block's panel in the season folder. The engine notes any position-day with a holding return beyond 5x so a human sees it.
Done when: tests show cleaning removes injected spikes but keeps real moves that don't revert; the pre-filtered universe equals the unfiltered one on the synthetic market; a discovery panel for the top 1,000 of the real archive loads in under a minute and fits in memory.
Notes: cleaning looks one day ahead to recognise a reversal, as vendors' cleaning does. It only touches physically implausible round trips; a real 3x move that sticks is kept. Recorded in docs/design.md.
Result: done, with one miss. The first load of the top-1,000 discovery panel takes about 105 s (peak ~4.9 GB); every later run reads the cached panel in a few seconds. Cleaning also gained a thin-junk rule, an absurd-price rule and split adjustment (see LEARNINGS).
