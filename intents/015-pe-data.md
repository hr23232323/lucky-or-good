# 015 P/E data, point in time
Status: active
Problem: strategies can only see prices and volume, and the question asked was "does P/E work, market-wide or stock by stock?". The price archive has no earnings. Earnings are the easiest place to leak the future: figures are published weeks after the period they describe, revised later, and the latest months in public datasets are estimates.
Outcome: the panel optionally carries `market` (monthly S&P 500 P/E and CAPE from Shiller, earnings lagged six months, each row dated when it became available, history back to the 1880s but nothing after the panel ends) and `earnings_yield` (per stock, trailing EPS from SEC filings counted from the day after filing, over that day's close; unknown after 15 months without a filing). A season opts in with `--fundamentals DIR`; `lucky data shiller` builds the market table.
Done when: tests show a month's value is invisible until the month is over, a changed earnings figure moves P/E only after the lag, stock EPS counts from its availability date, stale EPS goes unknown, and truncation keeps the new fields point in time. Stock-level data needs an SEC contact address from the human (the SEC requires one in the User-Agent).
Notes: Shiller's file is today's vintage, so any revisions to old S&P earnings are baked in. EDGAR XBRL starts in 2009-2011, so per-stock P/E exists only from about 2011.
